Better Experiences Start With Understanding the Problem

Great UX starts before the first screen is drawn

Great UX isn't simply about making software look better. It's about understanding what users are trying to accomplish, identifying what gets in their way, and designing a better path forward. JDZ Solutions approaches UI/UX from both the user and product perspective — considering usability, workflow, business requirements, technical realities, and the larger experience surrounding the interface.

UX for AI-Powered Products

AI changes what's possible. UX determines whether it's usable.

Generative AI introduces unique design challenges. Results may vary, processing can take time, users need ways to guide and refine outputs, and automated actions must remain understandable and controllable. JDZ Solutions combines hands-on AI and UX experience to design interactions around these realities — helping turn sophisticated AI capabilities into experiences people can confidently use.

Prompt & Input Experiences

Help users communicate intent without requiring them to become prompt engineers.

Generation & Feedback

Clearly communicate processing, progress, results, failures, and available next steps.

Human Control

Give users meaningful ways to review, edit, regenerate, override, and refine AI-generated results.

Complexity Management

Expose advanced capabilities when they're needed without overwhelming users who don't need them.

More Than Visual Design

UI is what users see. UX is everything they experience.

Visual quality matters, but a polished interface can't compensate for a confusing workflow. JDZ Solutions looks beyond colors, typography, and individual screens to consider navigation, behavior, feedback, terminology, consistency, responsiveness, accessibility, and the complete sequence of interactions required to accomplish a goal. The result is design grounded in how the product needs to work — not decoration applied after the fact.
