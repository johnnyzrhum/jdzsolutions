Beta Opportunities

Help Shape Emerging Media Solutions

JDZ Solutions works with selected organizations to evaluate emerging capabilities for video creation, advertising, personalization, e-commerce, multilingual content, and media automation.

Beta participants receive guided access to relevant capabilities and an opportunity to influence product direction through structured feedback.

Apply to Become a Beta Participant

What to Expect

Guided access, in exchange for structured feedback

Beta participation is a two-way arrangement. JDZ Solutions provides guided access to a relevant capability; participants help evaluate whether it works in a real workflow.

Who May Be a Good Fit

Organizations with a real, current use case for the capability being evaluated — not a general interest in trying new tools.

What Participation Looks Like

An introductory session, a defined test scenario, real or representative content, and structured feedback discussions.

What May Change

Features, availability, and timing may change during a beta. Applying does not guarantee acceptance or continued access.
